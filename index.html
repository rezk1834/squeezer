Fit a whole movie into 1 to 5 seconds.

Pick a video and get five silent time-lapses, 1 to 5 seconds long, each containing the entire film. Decoding happens with your browser's own video decoder, so nothing is uploaded and any file your browser can play will work.

Output format

Each video plays at 60 frames per second, so the 1 second clip holds 60 frames and the 5 second clip holds 300, picked at even intervals across the whole runtime. Audio is left out.
